# OCR benchmark — reading MPLADS site boards

44 boards drawn from real works in the portfolio, each put through one of eleven photograph conditions. The right answer is known exactly because each board was drawn from a known work. Generated by `scripts/benchmark_ocr.py` (seed 42).

**These are synthetic boards.** They measure the readers against the *kinds* of damage a phone photograph suffers — tilt, blur, fading, low light, noise, compression — not against real photographs. A sample of real boards, labelled by officers, replaces this the day one exists.

## Overall

| Reader | Work number exactly right | Any work number found | Amount within 1% | Seconds per board |

| RapidOCR (PP-OCRv4) | **91%** | 91% | 73% | 2.0 |

## Two readers on the same photograph

Both found a work number on 40 boards. They agreed on 39 and disagreed on 1. **They agreed on the same wrong number 0 time(s)** — the only case a cross-check cannot catch, and the reason every match is still confirmed by the officer.

At least one of Surya OCR 2 and RapidOCR (PP-OCRv4) read the number exactly right on 95% of boards.
